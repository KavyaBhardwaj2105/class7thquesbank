Section A — Multiple Choice Questions

Q1. Two line segments have the same length. Which term best describes their relationship?

Correct Answer: Congruent. Line segments of equal length are congruent.

Q2. Which transformation changes the position of a figure but preserves its shape and size?

Correct Answer: Translation.

Q3. A figure is turned through an angle while keeping its size unchanged. Which transformation has occurred?

Correct Answer: Rotation.

Q4. A mirror image of a shape is produced across a line. Which transformation is this?

Correct Answer: Reflection.

Q5. If △ABC and △PQR are congruent, which statement must be true?

Correct Answer: Corresponding sides and angles are equal.

Q6. Which pair of figures can be congruent even though one is turned upside down?

Correct Answer: Figures related by rotation.

Q7. Which condition is sufficient to prove two line segments congruent?

Correct Answer: Their lengths are equal.

Q8. A square is rotated by 90°. What happens to its size?

Correct Answer: It remains unchanged.

Q9. Which transformation always preserves distances between corresponding points?

Correct Answer: Reflection.

Q10. Two triangles have side lengths 5 cm, 7 cm and 9 cm each. What can be concluded?

Correct Answer: They are congruent by SSS.

Q11. If a triangle is reflected in a mirror line, what happens to the lengths of its sides?

Correct Answer: They remain unchanged.

Q12. Which pair of angles guarantees congruence of two triangles when the included side is also equal?

Correct Answer: ASA.

Q13. Which of the following is NOT generally enough by itself to establish triangle congruence?

Correct Answer: AAA. It establishes similarity, not necessarily congruence.

Q14. If corresponding sides of two triangles are equal, their corresponding angles are:

Correct Answer: Equal.

Q15. A rectangle measuring 8 cm × 5 cm is moved 6 cm to the right. What is its new area?

Correct Answer: 40 cm². Translation does not change dimensions.

Section B — True / False

Q16. Congruent figures have the same shape and the same size.

Correct Answer: True.

Q17. Rotating a figure changes its side lengths.

Correct Answer: False.

Q18. A reflected triangle has the same area as the original triangle.

Correct Answer: True.

Q19. Two squares of different side lengths can be congruent.

Correct Answer: False. Congruent squares must have equal side lengths.

Q20. Translation changes the shape of a figure.

Correct Answer: False.

Q21. If two triangles have all three corresponding sides equal, they are congruent.

Correct Answer: True — SSS criterion.

Q22. AAA alone always proves two triangles congruent.

Correct Answer: False. AAA establishes similarity, not necessarily equal size.

Q23. Reflection reverses the orientation of a figure.

Correct Answer: True.

Q24. Congruent figures always have equal perimeters.

Correct Answer: True.

Q25. A translation can change the area of a rectangle.

Correct Answer: False.

Section C — Assertion & Reasoning

A: Both A and R are true, and R explains A.
B: Both A and R are true, but R does not explain A.

Q26. Assertion: A translated figure is congruent to the original.
Reason: Translation preserves every distance and angle.

Correct Answer: A.

Q27. Assertion: Two triangles with equal corresponding sides are congruent.
Reason: SSS is a valid triangle congruence criterion.

Correct Answer: A.

Q28. Assertion: Two figures with equal areas must be congruent.
Reason: Congruent figures have equal areas.

Correct Answer: D. Equal area does not guarantee equal shape and size, but congruent figures do have equal areas.

Q29. Assertion: Reflection preserves the size of a figure.
Reason: Reflection preserves distances between corresponding points.

Correct Answer: A.

Q30. Assertion: AAA proves triangle congruence.
Reason: AAA gives equality of corresponding angles.

Correct Answer: D. AAA is true as an angle relationship, but it does not establish congruence without a size condition.

Section D — Fill in the Blanks

Q31. Figures having exactly the same shape and size are called ______ figures.

Correct Answer: Congruent.

Q32. Moving a figure without turning or changing its size is called a ______.

Correct Answer: Translation.

Q33. Turning a figure around a fixed point is called ______.

Correct Answer: Rotation.

Q34. The congruence criterion involving three corresponding sides is called ______.

Correct Answer: SSS.

Q35. A mirror image across a line is known as a ______.

Correct Answer: Reflection.

Section G — HOTS / Higher Order Thinking

Q46. Triangle ABC has sides 5 cm, 6 cm and 7 cm. Triangle PQR also has sides 5 cm, 6 cm and 7 cm. Can you conclude that the triangles are congruent?

Correct Answer: Yes, by SSS.

Q47. A rectangle is translated 12 cm upward. Its original length is 14 cm and width is 9 cm. What is its new area?

Correct Answer: 126 cm². Translation does not change dimensions.

Q48. Two triangles have two sides of lengths 7 cm and 9 cm. Their included angles are both 40°. What congruence criterion applies?

Correct Answer: SAS.

Q49. Two triangles have equal corresponding angles but one has sides exactly twice as long as the other. What is the best conclusion?

Correct Answer: They are similar but not congruent.

Q50. A shape is reflected and then translated. Which property definitely remains unchanged?

Correct Answer: Its side lengths.

Q51. A student says, “If two figures have the same perimeter, they must be congruent.” Which example disproves the claim?

Correct Answer: A 3×5 rectangle and a 4×4 square. Both have perimeter 16 cm, but their shapes and dimensions differ.

Q52. A triangle is rotated 180° about one of its vertices. What happens to the lengths of its three sides?

Correct Answer: All remain unchanged.

Q53. Two triangles have sides 8 cm, 10 cm and 12 cm. A second pair has sides 16 cm, 20 cm and 24 cm. What is their relationship?

Correct Answer: Similar but not congruent. Every corresponding side in the second triangle is twice the first.

Q54. A square has side 6 cm. It is reflected and then rotated. What is its perimeter after both transformations?

Correct Answer: 24 cm. Neither reflection nor rotation changes side length.

Q55. Triangle A has sides 4 cm, 5 cm and 6 cm. Triangle B has sides 4 cm, 5 cm and 7 cm. Which conclusion is correct?

Correct Answer: They cannot be congruent by SSS because one corresponding side differs.
