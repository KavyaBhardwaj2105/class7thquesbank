Section A — Multiple Choice Questions

Q1. What is the value of (−8) + 13?

Correct Answer: 5.

Q2. Which expression has the value −17?

Correct Answer: −12 − 5 = −17.

Q3. What is (−7) × (−6)?

Correct Answer: 42. The product of two negative integers is positive.

Q4. Find (−72) ÷ 9.

Correct Answer: −8.

Q5. Which integer is the additive inverse of −19?

Correct Answer: 19.

Q6. Which statement about multiplication of integers is always true?

Correct Answer: Negative × Negative is positive.

Q7. What is 15 − (−9)?

Correct Answer: 24. Subtracting a negative means adding its positive.

Q8. What is −4 + (−11) + 7?

Correct Answer: −8.

Q9. Which number is farthest from zero?

Correct Answer: −15, because its distance from zero is 15.

Correct Answer: −22.

Correct Answer: (−9) × 4 = −36.

Q12. A temperature changes from −6°C to 4°C. By how many degrees did it increase?

Correct Answer: 10°C.

Q13. If a × (−1) = −27, then a equals:

Correct Answer: 27.

Q14. What is the value of [−24 ÷ (−6)] − 9?

Correct Answer: −5. −24 ÷ −6 = 4, and 4 − 9 = −5.

Q15. Which integer must be added to −28 to obtain zero?

Correct Answer: 28.

Section B — True / False

Q16. The sum of two negative integers is always negative.

Correct Answer: True.

Q17. The difference of two positive integers is always positive.

Correct Answer: False. For example, 5 − 9 = −4.

Q18. Multiplying an integer by zero always gives zero.

Correct Answer: True.

Q19. The product of a positive and a negative integer is positive.

Correct Answer: False. The product is negative.

Q20. Every integer has an additive inverse.

Correct Answer: True.

Q21. −17 is greater than −9.

Correct Answer: False. −9 is greater because it is closer to zero.

Q22. Dividing a negative integer by a negative integer gives a positive result when the divisor is non-zero.

Correct Answer: True.

Q23. Subtracting an integer is equivalent to adding its additive inverse.

Correct Answer: True.

Q24. The additive inverse of zero is 1.

Correct Answer: False. The additive inverse of 0 is 0.

Q25. Integer multiplication is commutative.

Correct Answer: True. For integers a and b, a × b = b × a.

Section G — HOTS / Higher Order Thinking

Q46. A submarine is at −120 m. It rises 45 m and then descends 30 m. What is its final position?

Correct Answer: −105 m. −120 + 45 − 30 = −105.

Q47. A player gains 18 points in one round, loses 25 in the next and gains 12 in the third. What is the net change?

Correct Answer: 5 points. 18 − 25 + 12 = 5.

Q48. Without calculating every expression, which has the greatest value?

Correct Answer: −6 + 15 = 9, the greatest value.

Q49. Find the integer x if x − (−13) = −4.

Correct Answer: −17. x + 13 = −4, so x = −17.

Q50. Three consecutive integers have a sum of −18. What is the middle integer?

Correct Answer: −6. The integers are −7, −6 and −5.

Q51. A bank account has a balance of −₹450. A deposit of ₹700 is made, followed by a withdrawal of ₹325. What is the new balance?

Correct Answer: −₹75. −450 + 700 − 325 = −75.

Q52. If the product of two integers is −72 and one integer is −8, what is the other integer?

Correct Answer: 9. (−8) × 9 = −72.

Q53. A temperature is −3°C at 6 a.m. It falls by 8°C, then rises by 15°C. What is the final temperature?

Correct Answer: 4°C. −3 − 8 + 15 = 4.

Q54. Which statement explains why −5 × 7 and 5 × (−7) have the same value?

Correct Answer: Each has exactly one negative factor, so both products are −35.

Q55. An elevator starts at floor −2. It goes up 7 floors, down 4 floors, and then up 3 floors. At which floor does it stop?

Correct Answer: Floor 4. −2 + 7 − 4 + 3 = 4.
