SECTION A — 15 MULTIPLE CHOICE QUESTIONS
MCQ 1
Find the value of 18 + 6 × 4.
Correct Answer: B — 42
Multiplication is performed before addition: 18 + (6 × 4) = 18 + 24 = 42.
MCQ 2
What is the value of 48 ÷ 6 + 7 × 3?
Correct Answer: B — 29
48 ÷ 6 = 8 and 7 × 3 = 21. Therefore 8 + 21 = 29.
MCQ 3
Which expression represents: "Subtract 15 from the product of 8 and 9"?
Correct Answer: A
MCQ 4
Evaluate: 72 − [18 + (6 × 5)].
Correct Answer: 24
6 × 5 = 30; 18 + 30 = 48; 72 − 48 = 24.
MCQ 5
Which operation should be performed first in 35 − 12 ÷ 3 + 4?
Correct Answer: A
Division has priority over addition and subtraction.
MCQ 6
Find the value of 5 × (14 − 6) + 9.
Correct Answer: 49
14 − 6 = 8; 5 × 8 = 40; 40 + 9 = 49.
MCQ 7
Which expression has the value 50?
Correct Answer: B — 50
7 × 8 − 6 = 56 − 6 = 50.
MCQ 8
A student writes 20 + 5 × 2 = 50. What is the error?
Correct Answer: A
Correct value = 20 + (5 × 2) = 30.
MCQ 9
Evaluate: 100 − 4 × (7 + 3).
Correct Answer: A — 60
7 + 3 = 10; 4 × 10 = 40; 100 − 40 = 60.
MCQ 10
Which pair of brackets changes 6 + 4 × 5 into an expression with value 50?
Correct Answer: A
MCQ 11
A shopkeeper packs 6 boxes containing 24 pencils each and then removes 18 damaged pencils. Which expression gives the remaining pencils?
Correct Answer: A
MCQ 12
Which expression is equivalent to 45 + 30 ÷ 5?
Correct Answer: B
MCQ 13
If a = 12, what is the value of 3a + 7?
Correct Answer: B — 43
3 × 12 + 7 = 36 + 7 = 43.
MCQ 14
Which expression represents "twice the sum of 11 and 9"?
Correct Answer: A
MCQ 15
Find the value of 96 ÷ [4 × (2 + 2)].
Correct Answer: A — 6
2 + 2 = 4; 4 × 4 = 16; 96 ÷ 16 = 6.
SECTION B — 10 TRUE / FALSE
T/F 1
Multiplication generally has priority over addition in an arithmetic expression.
Correct Answer: TRUE
T/F 2
The value of 30 − 4 × 5 is 130.
Correct Answer: FALSE
4 × 5 = 20, so 30 − 20 = 10.
T/F 3
Brackets can change the value of an arithmetic expression.
Correct Answer: TRUE
T/F 4
In 18 ÷ 3 × 2, division must always be performed after multiplication.
Correct Answer: FALSE
Division and multiplication have the same priority and are evaluated from left to right.
T/F 5
The expression 7 × (3 + 2) is equal to 35.
Correct Answer: TRUE
T/F 6
The expressions 5 + 3 × 2 and (5 + 3) × 2 always have the same value.
Correct Answer: FALSE
The first is 11, while the second is 16.
T/F 7
A numerical expression can contain more than one operation.
Correct Answer: TRUE
T/F 8
24 ÷ (3 + 1) has value 10.
Correct Answer: FALSE
3 + 1 = 4 and 24 ÷ 4 = 6.
T/F 9
Changing the position of brackets can change the meaning of an expression.
Correct Answer: TRUE
T/F 10
In 50 − 8 + 3, addition must be done before subtraction.
Correct Answer: FALSE
Addition and subtraction have equal priority and are evaluated from left to right.
SECTION C — 5 ASSERTION & REASONING
A — Both Assertion and Reason are true and Reason correctly explains Assertion.
B — Both are true but Reason does not correctly explain Assertion.
C — Assertion is true but Reason is false.
D — Assertion is false but Reason is true.
A/R 1
Assertion: 8 + 4 × 3 = 20.
Reason: Multiplication is performed before addition.
Correct Answer: A
A/R 2
Assertion: (10 + 5) × 2 is greater than 10 + 5 × 2.
Reason: Brackets can force addition to be completed before multiplication.
Correct Answer: A
30 > 20, and the brackets explain why.
A/R 3
Assertion: 36 ÷ 6 × 2 = 3.
Reason: Division and multiplication have equal priority and are evaluated left to right.
Correct Answer: D
Assertion is false because 36 ÷ 6 × 2 = 6 × 2 = 12. Reason is true.
A/R 4
Assertion: 40 − (12 + 8) = 20.
Reason: The expression inside the brackets must be evaluated first.
Correct Answer: A
A/R 5
Assertion: 24 + 6 ÷ 3 = 10.
Reason: Addition is always performed before division.
Correct Answer: D
The assertion is false: 24 + 2 = 26. The reason is also false in general, but for this question the intended classification is corrected as: Assertion FALSE, Reason FALSE.

SECTION E — 5 MATCH THE FOLLOWING
Match each expression with its value.
1. 8 + 3 × 4
2. (8 + 3) × 4
3. 48 ÷ (4 + 2)
4. 30 − 2 × 5
Answers: 1 → 20, 2 → 44, 3 → 8, 4 → 20.
Match the verbal statement with its expression.
1. Add 7 to twice 5
2. Subtract 4 from 3 times 9
3. Triple the sum of 2 and 6
Answers: 1 → 2 × 5 + 7; 2 → 3 × 9 − 4; 3 → 3 × (2 + 6).
Match each expression with the operation that should be done first.
1. 18 + 7 × 2
2. (18 + 7) × 2
3. 45 ÷ 5 + 6
Answers: 1 → 7 × 2; 2 → 18 + 7; 3 → 45 ÷ 5.
Match each expression with an equivalent expression.
1. 5 × (8 + 2)
2. 40 + 12 ÷ 4
3. 7 × (10 − 3)
Answers: 1 → 5 × 8 + 5 × 2; 2 → 40 + 3; 3 → 70 − 21.
Match the situation with the correct expression.
1. 5 packets of 12 biscuits, 8 eaten
2. 60 candies shared among 5 children
3. 8 rows of 7 chairs plus 3 extra
Answers: 1 → 5 × 12 − 8; 2 → 60 ÷ 5; 3 → 8 × 7 + 3.

SECTION G — 10 HOTS QUESTIONS
HOTS 1
Riya calculates 60 − 12 ÷ 3 × 2. What is the correct value?
Correct Answer: A — 52
12 ÷ 3 = 4; 4 × 2 = 8; 60 − 8 = 52.
HOTS 2
Insert one pair of brackets in 8 + 4 × 3 so that the value becomes 36.
Correct Answer: A
HOTS 3
A teacher says that 48 ÷ (6 + 2) and 48 ÷ 6 + 2 have the same value. Is the statement correct?
Correct Answer: A
HOTS 4
A shop sells 4 notebooks at ₹35 each and gives a total discount of ₹20. Which expression gives the amount paid?
Correct Answer: A
Total price = 140; after ₹20 discount = ₹120.
HOTS 5
Find the missing number: 18 + □ × 4 = 46.
Correct Answer: A — 7
18 + 7 × 4 = 18 + 28 = 46.
HOTS 6
Two expressions are given: A = 5 × (12 − 4) B = 5 × 12 − 4. Which statement is correct?
Correct Answer: A
HOTS 7
A number is first multiplied by 4 and then 6 is added. The final result is 38. What was the original number?
Correct Answer: A — 8
4x + 6 = 38 → 4x = 32 → x = 8.
HOTS 8
Which pair of expressions has the same value?
Correct Answer: B
18 − 6 ÷ 3 = 18 − 2 = 16.
HOTS 9
A teacher asks students to create an expression equal to 25 using the numbers 5, 10 and 3 exactly once. Which option works?
Correct Answer: B — 25
10 × 3 − 5 = 30 − 5 = 25.
HOTS 10
An expression is written as: 72 ÷ 3 × (2 + 4) − 5. What is its value?
Correct Answer: 139
2 + 4 = 6; 72 ÷ 3 = 24; 24 × 6 = 144; 144 − 5 = 139.
