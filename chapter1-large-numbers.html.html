SECTION A — MULTIPLE CHOICE QUESTIONS
MCQ 1
The population of a city is 7,08,45,216. What is the place value of digit 8?
Correct Answer: A — 8,00,000
The digit 8 is in the lakh place.
MCQ 2
Which is the greatest number?
Correct Answer: B — 10,02,34,567
It has 10 crore, while the other two 9-crore numbers are smaller. Between the two 10-crore numbers, 02,34,567 is greater than 00,99,999.
MCQ 3
Which number is represented by 6 crore + 4 lakh + 8 thousand + 25?
Correct Answer: B — 6,04,08,025
60,000,000 + 400,000 + 8,000 + 25 = 60,408,025.
MCQ 4
In the International system, 4,305,018,072 is read as:
Correct Answer: B
4,305,018,072 = 4 billion + 305 million + 18 thousand + 72.
MCQ 5
What is 99,99,999 + 1?
Correct Answer: A/B — 1,00,00,000
99,99,999 is one less than one crore. Adding 1 gives exactly one crore.
MCQ 6
Which is the correct expanded form of 5,07,03,009?
Correct Answer: A
The digits occupy crore, lakh, thousand and ones places respectively.
MCQ 7
A number is 2 crore more than 8,75,43,210. What is the number?
Correct Answer: 8,95,43,210
8,75,43,210 + 2,00,00,000 = 10,75,43,210. Correction: therefore the correct option is D.
MCQ 8
Which number rounds to 45,00,000 when rounded to the nearest lakh?
Correct Answer: B — 44,76,500
The number is closer to 45 lakh than 44 lakh.
MCQ 9

Correct Answer: A
MCQ 10
Which statement is always true?
Correct Answer: B
MCQ 11
The difference between 7,50,00,000 and 6,99,99,999 is:
Correct Answer: 50,001
7,50,00,000 − 6,99,99,999 = 50,001.
MCQ 12
Which number is exactly halfway between 40,00,000 and 50,00,000?
Correct Answer: B — 45,00,000
MCQ 13
If 1 million = 10 lakh, then 35 million equals:
Correct Answer: B — 3.5 crore
MCQ 14
A company sold 12,48,750 units in one year and 15,76,250 in the next. How many more units were sold in the second year?
Correct Answer: B — 3,27,500
MCQ 15
A number becomes 6,00,00,000 when rounded to the nearest crore. Which interval contains all possible whole numbers that round to this value?
Correct Answer: B
Numbers from 5.5 crore up to but not including 6.5 crore round to 6 crore.
SECTION B — TRUE OR FALSE
T/F 1
1 crore is equal to 10 million.
Correct Answer: TRUE
T/F 2
The place value of 7 in 47,25,631 is 7,00,000.
Correct Answer: TRUE
T/F 3
10 lakh is greater than 1 crore.
Correct Answer: FALSE
1 crore = 10 lakh, so 10 lakh is equal to 1 crore.
T/F 4
The number 8,00,00,001 is greater than 80,00,000.
Correct Answer: TRUE
T/F 5
Every number ending in 000 becomes zero when rounded to the nearest thousand.
Correct Answer: TRUE
T/F 6
In the Indian system, commas are placed after every three digits from the right.
Correct Answer: FALSE
After the first three digits, commas occur after every two digits.
T/F 7
The successor of 99,99,999 is 1,00,00,000.
Correct Answer: TRUE
T/F 8
8,76,54,321 and 87,65,432 have the same number of digits.
Correct Answer: FALSE
The first has 8 digits and the second has 7 digits.
T/F 9
When comparing two numbers with the same number of digits, their leftmost digits should be compared first.
Correct Answer: TRUE
T/F 10
Rounding 7,49,999 to the nearest lakh gives 7,00,000.
Correct Answer: FALSE
7,49,999 is closer to 7,00,000 than 8,00,000? Difference from 7 lakh is 49,999, so it actually rounds to 7,00,000. Therefore the statement is TRUE.

SECTION G — HOTS / HIGHER ORDER THINKING
HOTS 1
A school has 4,85,750 students in its network. It wants to report the number rounded to the nearest lakh. A student reports 5,00,000 while another reports 4,00,000. Who is correct and why?
Correct Answer: A
HOTS 2
A number has 8 digits. Its crore digit is 3, its lakh digit is 7, its thousand digit is 4 and its last three digits form the smallest possible 3-digit number. Find the number.
Correct Answer: 3,07,04,100
The smallest 3-digit number is 100.
HOTS 3
Two populations are 4,99,99,999 and 5,00,00,001. Without performing ordinary subtraction first, determine their difference.
Correct Answer: 2
The first number is one less than 5 crore and the second is one more, so the gap is 2.
HOTS 4
A number is between 7 crore and 8 crore. Its lakh digit is 4 and its ten-thousand digit is 9. What can be said about the number?
Correct Answer: B
HOTS 5
A city claims that its population of 12,49,999 rounds to 12 lakh when rounded to the nearest lakh. Is the claim correct?
Correct Answer: A
HOTS 6
A library has 2,75,48,600 books distributed equally among 100 branches. Approximately how many books would each branch receive?
Correct Answer: 2,75,486
Dividing by 100 shifts the place values two places to the right.
HOTS 7
A number has exactly 8 digits. When its first digit is removed, the resulting number has 7 digits. What is the greatest possible original number if the remaining seven digits are all 9?
Correct Answer: 99,999,999
An 8-digit number cannot be formed by a single digit followed by seven 9s unless the first digit is also non-zero. The maximum is therefore 99,999,999.
HOTS 8
A newspaper reports a population as approximately 3.6 crore. Which of the following could be the exact population?
Correct Answer: B
3,57,80,000 is reasonably close to 3.6 crore.
HOTS 9
A number is written incorrectly as 8,45,32,100 instead of 8,54,32,100. By how much has the number been underestimated?
Correct Answer: 9,00,000
8,54,32,100 − 8,45,32,100 = 9,00,000.
HOTS 10
A number is rounded to the nearest crore and becomes 9 crore. Which statement must be true?
Correct Answer: A
All values from 8.5 crore through 9.499999... crore round to 9 crore.
