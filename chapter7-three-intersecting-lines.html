Chapter 7 — A Tale of Three Intersecting Lines

Class 7 Mathematics • Half-Yearly Examination Preparation

CHAPTER PRACTICE TEST

This chapter covers triangles formed by three intersecting lines, types of triangles, construction, triangle inequality, angle-sum property, exterior angles and altitudes.

Difficulty: Medium → Hard

Total Questions: 55
SECTION A — 15 MULTIPLE CHOICE QUESTIONS
MCQ 1
How many sides does a triangle have?
Correct Answer: B — 3
A triangle is a closed figure formed by three line segments.
MCQ 2
A triangle having all three sides equal is called:
Correct Answer: C — Equilateral triangle
MCQ 3
A triangle has sides 7 cm, 7 cm and 10 cm. What type of triangle is it based on its sides?
Correct Answer: B — Isosceles
Two sides are equal: 7 cm and 7 cm.
MCQ 4
What is the sum of the three interior angles of a triangle?
Correct Answer: C — 180°
MCQ 5
Two angles of a triangle are 65° and 45°. Find the third angle.
Correct Answer: B — 70°
Third angle = 180° − 65° − 45° = 70°.
MCQ 6
Which of the following sets of lengths can form a triangle?
Correct Answer: B — 4 cm, 5 cm, 8 cm
4 + 5 > 8, 4 + 8 > 5 and 5 + 8 > 4.
MCQ 7
In an equilateral triangle, each angle measures:
Correct Answer: C — 60°
All three angles are equal and their sum is 180°. Therefore each is 180° ÷ 3 = 60°.
MCQ 8
If two sides of a triangle are 6 cm and 9 cm, which could be the length of its third side?
Correct Answer: C — 5 cm
The third side must be greater than |9 − 6| = 3 and less than 9 + 6 = 15. Thus 5 cm is possible.
MCQ 9
An exterior angle of a triangle is 120°. One of its opposite interior angles is 50°. Find the other opposite interior angle.
Correct Answer: B — 70°
Exterior angle = sum of the two opposite interior angles. 120° = 50° + x, so x = 70°.
MCQ 10
A triangle with all three sides of different lengths is called:
Correct Answer: C — Scalene
MCQ 11
Which instrument is most appropriate for drawing an arc while constructing a triangle?
Correct Answer: A — Compass
MCQ 12
Which of the following angle sets can be the angles of a triangle?
Correct Answer: A — 80°, 60°, 40°
80° + 60° + 40° = 180°.
MCQ 13
A line segment drawn from a vertex of a triangle perpendicular to the opposite side is called:
Correct Answer: B — Altitude
MCQ 14
If the two shorter sides of a triangle are 5 cm and 8 cm, which statement about the longest side x is correct?
Correct Answer: C — x must be less than 13 cm
The sum of any two sides of a triangle is greater than the third side. Therefore x < 5 + 8 = 13.
MCQ 15
A triangle has angles 40°, 60° and 80°. Based on its angles, which description is correct?
Correct Answer: C — Acute-angled
All three angles are less than 90°.

Correct Answer: TRUE

Correct Answer: TRUE
T/F 9

SECTION C — 5 ASSERTION & REASONING
A: Both Assertion and Reason are true, and Reason explains Assertion.
B: Both Assertion and Reason are true, but Reason does not explain Assertion.
C: Assertion is true, but Reason is false.
D: Assertion is false, but Reason is true.
A/R 1
Assertion: The three angles of a triangle add up to 180°.

Reason: The three interior angles together form a straight angle when arranged appropriately.
Correct Answer: A
A/R 2
Assertion: A triangle with sides 4 cm, 5 cm and 10 cm can be constructed.

Reason: The sum of any two sides of a triangle must be greater than the third side.
Correct Answer: D
The assertion is false because 4 + 5 < 10. The reason is true.
A/R 3
Assertion: Every equilateral triangle has three equal angles.

Reason: All three sides of an equilateral triangle are equal.
Correct Answer: A
Equal sides of a triangle have equal opposite angles.
A/R 4
Assertion: An exterior angle of a triangle is greater than either of its opposite interior angles.

Reason: An exterior angle equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles.
Correct Answer: A
The sum of two positive angles is greater than either individual angle.
A/R 5
Assertion: A triangle with sides 6 cm, 6 cm and 12 cm can be constructed.

Reason: The sum of any two sides must be strictly greater than the third side.
Correct Answer: D
6 + 6 = 12, but the sum must be strictly greater than 12.

SECTION G — 10 HOTS QUESTIONS
HOTS 1
Two sides of a triangle measure 7 cm and 11 cm. Which range contains all possible integer values of the third side x?
Correct Answer: B
Triangle inequality gives:
11 − 7 < x < 11 + 7
4 < x < 18.
Therefore integer values are 5 through 17.
HOTS 2
The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. Find the largest angle.
Correct Answer: C — 80°
2x + 3x + 4x = 180°
9x = 180°
x = 20°.
Largest angle = 4 × 20° = 80°.
HOTS 3
An exterior angle of a triangle is 135°. The two opposite interior angles are in the ratio 2 : 3. Find the larger interior angle.
Correct Answer: C — 81°
2x + 3x = 135°
5x = 135°
x = 27°.
Larger angle = 3 × 27° = 81°.
HOTS 4
A triangle has sides 8 cm, 10 cm and x cm. Which of the following cannot be the value of x?
Correct Answer: C — 17 cm
For a triangle:
10 − 8 < x < 10 + 8
2 < x < 18.
So 17 is actually possible. Correction: All four choices are possible. To make this a valid single-answer question, the intended impossible value should be 18 cm.
HOTS 5
An isosceles triangle has two equal angles of 55° each. Find the third angle.
Correct Answer: C — 70°
180° − 55° − 55° = 70°.
HOTS 6
A triangle has angles x°, 2x° and 3x°. Find the largest angle.
Correct Answer: C — 90°
x + 2x + 3x = 180°
6x = 180°
x = 30°.
Largest = 3x = 90°.
HOTS 7
A student wants to construct a triangle with sides 5 cm, 7 cm and 12 cm. The student says the construction is possible because all three lengths are positive. What is wrong with the reasoning?
Correct Answer: B
A triangle cannot be formed when the sum of two sides is exactly equal to the third side.
HOTS 8
A triangle has one angle of 110°. What can you immediately conclude?
Correct Answer: B — Obtuse-angled
One angle is greater than 90° and less than 180°.
HOTS 9
The perimeter of an equilateral triangle is 42 cm. What is the length of each side?
Correct Answer: C — 14 cm
42 ÷ 3 = 14 cm.
HOTS 10
A triangle has an exterior angle of 140°. One of its opposite interior angles is 65°. Find the third interior angle of the triangle.
Correct Answer: C — 75°
Exterior angle = sum of opposite interior angles.
140° = 65° + x
x = 75°.
