Section G — HOTS / Higher Order Thinking (10)

46. A number is multiplied by 4 and then increased by 7. The result is 31. What is the original number?

Correct Answer: 6. Equation: 4x + 7 = 31 → 4x = 24 → x = 6.

47. The sum of two consecutive whole numbers is 41. Which pair are the numbers?

Correct Answer: 20 and 21.

48. A rectangle has length 3 cm more than its breadth. Its perimeter is 30 cm. What are its dimensions?

Correct Answer: 9 cm × 6 cm. If breadth = x, length = x + 3. 2(x + x + 3) = 30 → 4x + 6 = 30 → x = 6.

49. Ravi thinks of a number. He doubles it, subtracts 9, and gets 25. What number did he think of?

Correct Answer: 17. 2x − 9 = 25 → 2x = 34 → x = 17.

50. Three times a number is 12 more than the number itself. Find the number.

Correct Answer: 6. 3x = x + 12 → 2x = 12 → x = 6.

51. The perimeter of a triangle is 42 cm. Two sides are 13 cm and 15 cm. Find the third side.

Correct Answer: 14 cm.

52. A shopkeeper has some pencils. After giving 18 pencils away, 24 pencils remain. How many pencils were there originally?

Correct Answer: 42. x − 18 = 24 → x = 42.

53. A number is divided by 5 and then 3 is added. The result is 11. Find the number.

Correct Answer: 40. x/5 + 3 = 11 → x/5 = 8 → x = 40.

54. The average of three numbers is 18. Two of the numbers are 14 and 21. What is the third number?

Correct Answer: 19. Total = 18 × 3 = 54; third number = 54 − 14 − 21 = 19.

55. A father is 4 times as old as his son. Their ages together are 50 years. What are their ages?

Correct Answer: Son = 10 years, Father = 40 years. If son = x, father = 4x. x + 4x = 50 → 5x = 50 → x = 10.
