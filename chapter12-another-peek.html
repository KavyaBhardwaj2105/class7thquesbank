Section G — HOTS / Higher Order Thinking (10)

46. Riya walked 2.75 km in the morning and 3.6 km in the evening. How far did she walk altogether?

Correct Answer: 6.35 km. 2.75 + 3.60 = 6.35.

47. A shopkeeper has 10 kg of rice. He sells 3.75 kg in the morning and 2.8 kg in the afternoon. How much rice remains?

Correct Answer: 3.45 kg. 10 − 3.75 − 2.80 = 3.45 kg.

48. A number is represented as 4 + 6/10 + 2/100 + 5/1000. Which decimal represents it?

Correct Answer: 4.625.

49. Which is the smallest decimal greater than 6.48 among the following?

Correct Answer: 6.481

50. A tank contains 18.5 L of water. If 6.75 L is used, what percentage of the original quantity remains expressed as a decimal of the original?

Correct Answer: About 0.6351. Remaining = 18.5 − 6.75 = 11.75 L. 11.75 ÷ 18.5 ≈ 0.6351.

51. A rectangular garden is 7.5 m long and 4.2 m wide. What is its area?

Correct Answer: 31.5 m². 7.5 × 4.2 = 31.5.

52. A student claims that 5.09 is greater than 5.9 because 09 is greater than 9. What is the correct conclusion?

Correct Answer: The claim is wrong. 5.90 > 5.09.

53. A wire is 12.6 m long. It is cut into pieces of 0.9 m each. How many complete pieces can be made?

Correct Answer: 14 pieces. 12.6 ÷ 0.9 = 14.

54. Four students record their jump distances: A = 2.35 m, B = 2.305 m, C = 2.53 m, D = 2.5 m. Who jumps the farthest?

Correct Answer: C, because 2.53 is the greatest distance.

55. A 20 m rope is divided into three parts measuring 6.75 m, 5.8 m and x m. What is x?

Correct Answer: 7.45 m. x = 20 − 6.75 − 5.80 = 7.45 m.
