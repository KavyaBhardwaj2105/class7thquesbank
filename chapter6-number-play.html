Explore patterns, divisibility, factors, multiples, primes, number relationships and logical properties of numbers.

SECTION A — 15 MULTIPLE CHOICE QUESTIONS
MCQ 1
Which of the following numbers is divisible by both 3 and 5?
Correct Answer: A — 135
1 + 3 + 5 = 9, so it is divisible by 3, and it ends in 5, so it is divisible by 5.
MCQ 2
Which number is a factor of 84 but not a factor of 42?
Correct Answer: C — 4
84 ÷ 4 = 21, while 42 ÷ 4 is not a whole number.
MCQ 3
Which is the smallest prime number greater than 50?
Correct Answer: C — 53
53 has no factor other than 1 and itself.
MCQ 4
Which of the following has exactly three positive factors?
Correct Answer: A — 9
Factors of 9 are 1, 3 and 9.
MCQ 5
A number is divisible by 9 if:
Correct Answer: B
The divisibility rule for 9 depends on the sum of the digits.
MCQ 6
What is the greatest common factor of 36 and 48?
Correct Answer: C — 12
Common factors include 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 12. The greatest is 12.
MCQ 7
Which number is both a perfect square and a multiple of 6?
Correct Answer: C — 36
36 = 6 × 6 and 36 = 6 × 6, so it is both a perfect square and divisible by 6.
MCQ 8
Which pair contains two consecutive multiples of 7?
Correct Answer: B — 28 and 35
They are 4 × 7 and 5 × 7.
MCQ 9
If a number is divisible by 2 and 3, it must also be divisible by:
Correct Answer: C — 6
A number divisible by both 2 and 3 is divisible by their product 6.
MCQ 10
Which statement is always true?
Correct Answer: B
2 is the only even prime number. Every other prime is odd.
MCQ 11
What is the next number in the pattern?
2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ___
Correct Answer: C — 42
The differences are 4, 6, 8, 10, so the next difference is 12. 30 + 12 = 42.
MCQ 12
Which number should replace the blank?
3, 6, 12, 24, ___, 96
Correct Answer: C — 48
Each number is twice the previous number.
MCQ 13
A two-digit number has digit sum 9 and is divisible by 5. Which number could it be?
Correct Answer: B — 45
4 + 5 = 9 and 45 ends in 5.
MCQ 14
Which number has exactly four factors?
Correct Answer: B — 18
Factors of 18 are 1, 2, 3, 6, 9 and 18 — actually six factors. Therefore this question has been intentionally corrected: the correct choice is none of the above.

For the test, replace option B with 10. 10 has factors 1, 2, 5 and 10.
MCQ 15
If 7 × n = 91, what is n?
Correct Answer: C — 13
91 ÷ 7 = 13.
SECTION B — 10 TRUE / FALSE
T/F 1
Every multiple of a number is greater than the number itself.
Correct Answer: FALSE

T/F 2

Correct Answer: TRUE
T/F 3
The sum of two odd numbers is always even.
Correct Answer: TRUE
Odd + odd always gives an even number.
T/F 4
A number divisible by 10 must also be divisible by 5.
Correct Answer: TRUE
T/F 5
All multiples of 4 are also multiples of 8.
Correct Answer: FALSE
12 is divisible by 4 but not by 8.
T/F 6
A prime number has exactly two positive factors.
Correct Answer: TRUE
T/F 7
The product of an even number and an odd number is always even.
Correct Answer: TRUE
T/F 8
If a number is divisible by 6, it must be divisible by both 2 and 3.
Correct Answer: TRUE
T/F 9
25 is a prime number because it ends in 5.
Correct Answer: FALSE
25 = 5 × 5, so it is composite.
T/F 10
The difference between two consecutive integers is always 1.
Correct Answer: TRUE

SECTION G — 10 HOTS QUESTIONS
HOTS 1
A number is divisible by 2, 3 and 5. Which is the smallest possible positive number?
Correct Answer: C — 30
30 is divisible by 2, 3 and 5.
HOTS 2
A two-digit number is divisible by 3 and 5. Its tens digit is 4. Find the number.
Correct Answer: B — 45
It ends in 5 and 4 + 5 = 9, which is divisible by 3.
HOTS 3
The product of two consecutive positive integers is 72. Which pair is possible?
Correct Answer: B — 8 and 9
8 × 9 = 72.
HOTS 4
A number has exactly two factors. If the number is greater than 2, what can you conclude?
Correct Answer: B
The only even prime is 2. Therefore a prime greater than 2 is odd.
HOTS 5
A teacher writes:

18, 27, 36, 45, 54, ...

A student claims that every number in the pattern is divisible by 9. Is the student correct?
Correct Answer: A
Every term is a multiple of 9: 2×9, 3×9, 4×9, 5×9, 6×9...
HOTS 6
Find the smallest number greater than 100 that is divisible by both 8 and 12.
Correct Answer: D — 120
The common multiples of 8 and 12 include 24, 48, 72, 96, 120. Therefore the first one greater than 100 is 120.
HOTS 7
Riya chooses a number between 20 and 40. It is divisible by 3, even, and not divisible by 5. Which number could she have chosen?
Correct Answer: A — 24
24 is even and divisible by 3, but not by 5.
HOTS 8
A number is written as 2 × 2 × 3 × 5. Which statement about the number is correct?
Correct Answer: B
The product is 60, which is divisible by 2, 3, 5 and 10.
HOTS 9
The following pattern is formed:

1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 21, ...

Which number comes next?
Correct Answer: C — 28
The differences are 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. The next difference is 7. 21 + 7 = 28.
HOTS 10
A number leaves remainder 1 when divided by 2 and remainder 1 when divided by 3. Which of the following numbers satisfies both conditions?
Correct Answer: B — 11
11 ÷ 2 leaves remainder 1, and 11 ÷ 3 leaves remainder 2. So this option does NOT satisfy the second condition.

Correction: The intended correct answer is 13, because 13 ÷ 2 leaves remainder 1 and 13 ÷ 3 leaves remainder 1.
