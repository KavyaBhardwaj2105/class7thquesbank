Section A — Multiple Choice Questions

Q1. Which fraction is equivalent to 18/24 in its simplest form?

Correct Answer: 3/4. Divide numerator and denominator by 6.

Q2. Which is greater?

Correct Answer: 5/8. Using denominator 24: 7/12 = 14/24 while 5/8 = 15/24.

Q3. What is 3/5 + 7/10?

Correct Answer: 1 3/10.

Q4. Which fraction represents 2 wholes and 3/7?

Correct Answer: 17/7. 2 × 7 + 3 = 17.

Q5. What is 4/9 × 27/8?

Correct Answer: 3/2.

Q6. The reciprocal of 7/11 is:

Correct Answer: 11/7.

Q7. Which expression has the value 5/6?

Correct Answer: 1 − 1/6 = 5/6.

Q8. What is 2 1/4 − 5/8?

Correct Answer: 1 5/8.

Q9. Which fraction lies between 1/3 and 1/2?

Correct Answer: 5/12.

Q10. A quantity is divided into 8 equal parts. Five parts are taken. What fraction remains?

Correct Answer: 3/8.

Q11. What is 3/4 ÷ 2?

Correct Answer: 3/8. Dividing by 2 means multiplying by 1/2.

Q12. Which pair represents the same fraction?

Correct Answer: 5/6 and 15/18.

Q13. If 2/5 of a number is 18, what is the number?

Correct Answer: 45.

Q14. Which statement is correct?

Correct Answer: Every proper positive fraction is less than 1.

Q15. What is the value of 5/6 − 1/4?

Correct Answer: 7/12.

Section B — True / False

Q16. 4/7 and 12/21 are equivalent fractions.

Correct Answer: True.

Q17. 9/10 is smaller than 7/8.

Correct Answer: False. 9/10 = 36/40 while 7/8 = 35/40.

Q18. The product of two proper positive fractions is always less than each of them.

Correct Answer: True.

Q19. 7/3 is a proper fraction.

Correct Answer: False. The numerator is greater than the denominator.

Q20. Dividing a non-zero fraction by its reciprocal gives 1.

Correct Answer: False. A fraction divided by its reciprocal is generally not 1.

Q21. 3/5 = 15/25.

Correct Answer: True.

Q22. 1/6 + 1/6 + 1/6 = 1/2.

Correct Answer: True.

Q23. Multiplying a fraction by 1 leaves its value unchanged.

Correct Answer: True.

Q24. 11/12 − 5/12 = 1/2.

Correct Answer: True.

Q25. The reciprocal of 1 is 0.

Correct Answer: False. The reciprocal of 1 is 1.

Q28. Assertion: 7/9 + 2/9 = 1.
Reason: Fractions with equal denominators can be added by adding their numerators.

Correct Answer: B. Both statements are true, but the reason alone does not specifically establish that the sum equals 1 unless the numerators are added to get 9/9.

Q29. Assertion: 5/6 ÷ 2 = 5/12.

Correct Answer: A.

Q30. Assertion: 4/9 is greater than 1/2.

Correct Answer: D. The assertion is false, while the reason is true.

Section G — HOTS / Higher Order Thinking

Q46. Riya ate 2/7 of a cake and Aman ate 3/14 of the same cake. What fraction of the cake was eaten altogether?

Correct Answer: 1/2. 2/7 = 4/14, so 4/14 + 3/14 = 7/14 = 1/2.

Q47. A tank is 5/6 full. If 1/3 of the tank's total capacity is used, what fraction of the tank remains full?

Correct Answer: 1/2. 5/6 − 1/3 = 5/6 − 2/6 = 3/6 = 1/2.

Q48. A student claims that 5/8 + 2/3 = 7/11 because both numerators and denominators were added. What is the correct response?

Correct Answer: The denominators must first be made common. The actual result is 31/24.

Q49. Which is the smallest fraction among 5/6, 7/9, 11/15 and 13/18?

Correct Answer: 13/18.

Q50. A ribbon 7 1/2 m long is cut into pieces of 1/2 m each. How many pieces are obtained?

Correct Answer: 15. 7 1/2 = 15/2; (15/2) ÷ (1/2) = 15.

Q51. Find the missing fraction: 3/4 + □ = 1 1/8.

Correct Answer: 3/8. 1 1/8 = 9/8, and 9/8 − 6/8 = 3/8.

Q52. A number is multiplied by 4/5 and the result is 28. What was the original number?

Correct Answer: 35. 28 ÷ 4/5 = 28 × 5/4 = 35.

Q53. A path is 12 m long. One person walks 3/8 of it and another walks 1/4 of it. What total distance has been walked?

Correct Answer: 7.5 m. 3/8 + 1/4 = 5/8, and 5/8 × 12 = 60/8 = 7.5.

Q54. Without converting to decimals, decide which is greater: 17/24 or 5/7.

Correct Answer: 5/7. Cross multiplication: 17×7 = 119, while 5×24 = 120.

Q55. A recipe needs 3/4 cup of flour for one batch. How much flour is needed for 2 2/3 batches?

Correct Answer: 2 cups. 3/4 × 8/3 = 2.
