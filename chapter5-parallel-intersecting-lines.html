Chapter 5 — Parallel and Intersecting Lines

Class 7 Mathematics • Half-Yearly Examination Preparation

CHAPTER PRACTICE TEST

This chapter tests understanding of lines, line segments, rays, parallel lines, intersecting lines, perpendicular lines, angles, transversals and geometric reasoning.

Difficulty: Medium → Hard

Total Questions: 55
SECTION A — 15 MULTIPLE CHOICE QUESTIONS
MCQ 1
Which statement best describes parallel lines?
Correct Answer: B
Parallel lines lie in the same plane and do not intersect, however far they are extended.
MCQ 2
Two lines intersect at an angle of 90°. What type of lines are they?
Correct Answer: C — Perpendicular lines
MCQ 3
How many points are required to determine a unique straight line?
Correct Answer: B — Two
Exactly one straight line can be drawn through any two distinct points.
MCQ 4
A line that intersects two or more lines at different points is called a:
Correct Answer: B — Transversal
MCQ 5
If two lines have no common point even after being extended indefinitely, they are:
Correct Answer: C — Parallel
MCQ 6
Which pair of objects is the best real-life example of parallel lines?
Correct Answer: B — Opposite edges of a ruler
MCQ 7
If one angle formed by two perpendicular lines is 90°, what is the measure of each of the other three angles?
Correct Answer: C — 90°
MCQ 8
Which pair of lines can have exactly one common point?
Correct Answer: B — Intersecting lines
MCQ 9
Two lines intersect and one of the angles is 125°. What is the measure of its vertically opposite angle?
Correct Answer: C — 125°
Vertically opposite angles are equal.
MCQ 10
Two adjacent angles on a straight line are 72° and x°. Find x.
Correct Answer: B — 108°
Angles on a straight line add to 180°. 180° − 72° = 108°.
MCQ 11
Which pair of lines is guaranteed to remain parallel?
Correct Answer: A
MCQ 12
If a transversal crosses two parallel lines, which statement is true for corresponding angles?
Correct Answer: B — They are equal.
MCQ 13
A pair of parallel lines is cut by a transversal. If one corresponding angle is 64°, what is the other corresponding angle?
Correct Answer: B — 64°
MCQ 14
Which statement about perpendicular lines is always true?
Correct Answer: C — They meet at right angles.
MCQ 15
Three distinct lines are drawn in a plane. What is the maximum number of intersection points they can have?
Correct Answer: B — 3
Each pair of the three lines can intersect at a different point, giving a maximum of 3 intersection points.

SECTION C — 5 ASSERTION & REASONING
A: Both Assertion and Reason are true, and Reason explains Assertion.
B: Both Assertion and Reason are true, but Reason does not explain Assertion.
C: Assertion is true, but Reason is false.
D: Assertion is false, but Reason is true.
A/R 1
Assertion: Two distinct parallel lines do not intersect.
Reason: Parallel lines remain at the same distance from each other.
Correct Answer: A
A/R 2
Assertion: Perpendicular lines form four right angles.
Reason: A right angle measures 90°.
Correct Answer: A
A/R 3
Assertion: Two lines intersecting at 60° are perpendicular.
Reason: Perpendicular lines must intersect at 90°.
Correct Answer: D
The assertion is false, while the reason is true.
A/R 4
Assertion: Vertically opposite angles are equal.
Reason: Each pair of adjacent angles on a straight line sums to 180°.
Correct Answer: B
Both statements are true, but the stated reason alone does not directly explain the equality of vertically opposite angles without an additional angle relationship.

SECTION G — 10 HOTS QUESTIONS
HOTS 1
Two intersecting lines form one angle of 47°. Find the measures of the other three angles.
Correct Answer: A
The vertically opposite angle is 47° and each adjacent angle is 180° − 47° = 133°.
HOTS 2
Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One angle is 72°. How many of the eight angles are 72°?
Correct Answer: C — 4
Four angles are equal to the given acute angle and the remaining four are 108°.
HOTS 3
A road crosses two railway tracks. The tracks remain equally spaced throughout. What can you conclude about the railway tracks?
Correct Answer: B — Parallel
HOTS 4
Three lines are drawn. Line A is parallel to line B, and line B is parallel to line C. What can be concluded about A and C?
Correct Answer: B
Lines parallel to the same line are parallel to each other.
HOTS 5
An angle and its adjacent angle form a straight line. The first angle is three times the second. Find the smaller angle.
Correct Answer: B — 45°
Let smaller angle = x. Then 3x + x = 180°. 4x = 180°, so x = 45°.
HOTS 6
A student draws two lines and says they are perpendicular because they intersect. Is the reasoning sufficient?
Correct Answer: B
HOTS 7
Two adjacent angles are 5x and 3x. They form a linear pair. Find x.
Correct Answer: B — 22.5°
5x + 3x = 180°. 8x = 180°. x = 22.5°.
HOTS 8
Can two perpendicular lines also be considered intersecting lines?
Correct Answer: A
Perpendicular lines are a special case of intersecting lines. They intersect at 90°.
HOTS 9
Four distinct lines are drawn in a plane. What is the greatest possible number of intersection points if no two lines are parallel and no three lines pass through the same point?
Correct Answer: C — 6
Every pair of lines creates one intersection: 4 × 3 ÷ 2 = 6.
HOTS 10
A transversal cuts two parallel lines. One pair of interior angles on the same side of the transversal has measures x and 110°. Find x.
Correct Answer: B — 70°
Co-interior angles between parallel lines are supplementary. x + 110° = 180°, therefore x = 70°.
